# Launch Control — team sterlings

Presentation for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** (challenge: *Space Mission Design Game*).

**Open it:** https://gaelflores04.github.io/launch-control-sterlings/

**Project documentation (PDF):** https://gaelflores04.github.io/launch-control-sterlings/docs/launch-control-documentation.pdf

Works on phones, tablets and computers. Use the arrows, tap the right/left side or swipe to move between steps; on a phone, landscape gives the largest view. On a keyboard: Left/Right arrows, 1–5 to jump to a slide, F for fullscreen, P to play the video on slide 5.

Launch Control is a cooperative game prototype built in Godot: one player is the astronaut, the other is mission control, and they must share what they know to get the rocket launched before the countdown ends.

**AI disclosure:** code, scene construction, documentation and this presentation were developed with AI assistance (OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT and Anthropic Claude/Claude Code). Gameplay captures come from the team's prototype and are labelled as AI-assisted visuals. No NASA logos or insignia are used. NASA links are educational references, not live data.
